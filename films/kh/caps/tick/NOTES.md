## Score
The signature is a pencil tick (two dry clicks, pitched per step) for each tick, and a bright coin chime for each coin landing. The problem motif is the chime doubled and slightly out of time. A crash is a snap, a thud and a scrape back. The skip is a whoosh with the ticks read back softly. The wrong coin gives a dull wooden note and the jar's ✕ a thunk. The payoff ✓ is a three-note glass chord, and the pad enters only after the final ✓. Output is −16.1 LUFS with a true peak of −1.2 dBTP.

## Known weaknesses
- The limit reads as "the coin missed the jar". That is a fair everyday "wrong step", but a viewer may take it as a second kind of crash rather than "the step did the wrong thing and was still ticked".
- The title hinge is about 2.5 s under the veil (the same as gate). The whale moves under it, but it is the quietest stretch.
- The whale's peak screen speed is 29.5 px/frame at 21.98 s (the payoff hop during the camera release), just inside the 30 px limit.
- The "Crash!" chip sits across the gap between two cards and overlaps their edges for about 1 s.
- The case card paraphrases "0 duplicate applies" as "nothing done twice". That is accurate for the demo, but it is the project's own README claim, not an independent test.
