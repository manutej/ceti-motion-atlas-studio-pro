Station words (mono rail at the bottom, active one on a paper chip with a violet underline, plus a Fraunces headline and gloss at top left): Decision · Collapse · Witness · Split · Green. The glosses are verbatim from RESEARCH §5.
Example: checkout success, labelled "checkout success · illustrative" in the top-right chrome from 2.6 s to the lockup. The chip says "99%" (the rounded dashboard reading) rather than 99.1/99.2, to keep the "exactly the same" read honest and simple.

## Score signature
Before the split, both worlds' pulses land on the **same note** (E5, centre). That unison is the collapse. On the split, a held unison glides apart into C#5 (A, left) and G#5 (B, right), and from then on each world lands on its own note. The pad is a held E until the split, then it opens into A-maj7. The gap gets a sonar ping and a bell with a beating semitone partner. The alert is a soft three-note figure. Raw mix: about −19.7 LUFS, peak −1.9 dBFS; build.sh normalises it.

## Known weaknesses
- The unzip moment is about 1.1 s, and the motion blur of the strands near the chips looks briefly doubled.
- World labels are 17 px mono and shrink to about 16 px at the final zoom.
- While the strand chips cross-fade "99%" → new labels (about 0.4 s), the two texts overlap.
- The witness hold (11.4–12.2 s) is the quietest moment. The whale only bobs.
