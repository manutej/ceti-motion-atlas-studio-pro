## Known weaknesses
- The swapped "3 2" is on screen from frame 0 and nobody remarks on it until 21 s. That is the point of the limit beat, but a viewer may read it as a mistake in the film.
- The pod is still a fish shape; the roles are carried by labels and colour (fixer = pale teal, customer = driftwood).
- DUR is 30.0 s, at the top of the 25–30 s range; read-time floors on 9 captions set the length.
- The title keeps BATCH2's "try to break it". Its "it" refers to the report named 4 s earlier (O2 rule 5).
