## Two strongest
1. **sb-05-02 (counter hits zero).** The real ledger (14 · 14 · 10 · 0) counts down to the move itself, so CASE *is* the mechanism at full scale. The limit is pictured, not captioned: readers of the same colour share one blind spot. That carries RESEARCH.md's caution that a fixpoint is not the optimum.
2. **sb-05-08 (keep the judge pinned).** It names the one RSI trap that matters, a loop that edits its own judge, with a picture a stranger reads with the sound off: the whale widening the gate posts. The payoff (shove the pinned post, it won't budge) undoes FELT exactly. It is weak on numbers (rule only, no run).

Weakest overall: sb-05-06. Its case is a configured setting rather than a measured saving.
