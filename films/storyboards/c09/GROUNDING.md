Accuracy cautions carried into the meta-prompt:
- Every gate score in these repos is the author's own. The films must never say "verified by others".
- The colour-to-chord mapping is fixed and repeatable. It is not a judge of beauty: a sweet chord does not make a good palette ("Beauty is not a gate.").
- Passing a contrast check between text and paper says nothing about whether chart colours can be told apart.
- In Hekat, 99.7% is an estimate that assumes failures are independent. It does not go on screen.
- Sound written as code is only as good as someone's ears. The model cannot hear it.
