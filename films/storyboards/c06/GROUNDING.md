## Accuracy cautions (hard limits for every board)
- A type guarantee is not a correctness guarantee (JEV-works). A matching label can carry wrong content.
- "Filter, not oracle." A confident score is not a right answer (empty input scored 0.82–0.91).
- JEV is not JAX, gradients, FOL or a Lean kernel. Never say "proof" or "proven".
- `typesafe-ai/jev` is a hosted vendor model. Manu's repos are the gate, workbench, tape and lab around it. Don't say Manu built the model.
- The thresholds are design settings ("TypeSafe confidence does not write τ"). They are not discovered constants.
- Third-party claims (for example the router's "+82%" and the catalogue counts) are not re-verified. Keep them off screen.
- The Notion placement is honest: JEV "would not enter the Twenty today". Don't oversell it as a breakthrough.
