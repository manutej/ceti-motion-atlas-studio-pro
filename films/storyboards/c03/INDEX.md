## Two strongest
- **sb-03-09 AI writes. The gate checks.** This is the most widely felt pain right now (an AI filled the wiki overnight). It shows the whole inner loop in plain pictures: small batches, a machine check, a fix, three tries, then it asks you. Its ending (a towering pile becomes a short list of two flags) is the clearest callback in the set.
- **sb-03-03 The same mistake, pinned.** Its move is the one this cluster owns that no other film shows: a mistake found after saving becomes a hollow pin, proves itself, and then turns solid. The case is a real, verified escape (declared IBM Plex, rendered Inter), and the ending is a direct visual undo of FELT (the smudge bounces off at the same spot).
